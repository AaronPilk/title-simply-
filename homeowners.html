The basics

What is title insurance?

Title insurance protects both you and your mortgage lender from losses stemming from defects in the property's title.

Defects include improperly recorded documents, forgery, easements, and encroachments. Without it, buyers risk substantial financial liability for issues that existed before they ever owned the home.

One-time cost

A one-time, up-front fee — not an ongoing expense. Combined owner and lender policies typically cost 0.5–1.0% of the purchase price.

Lasting protection

Roughly $1,500–$3,000 on a $300,000 home, per ALTA. You pay once, and protection lasts as long as you own the property.

Good to know

Exclusions

Title insurance doesn't protect homeowners against every possible infringement on their property rights. It excludes issues arising from your own actions — such as unpaid contractors or tax defaults — as well as eminent domain seizures.
